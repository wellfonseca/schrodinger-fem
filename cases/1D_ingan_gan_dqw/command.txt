1D_ingan_gan_dqw  --  symmetric InGaN/GaN double quantum well

Benchmark taken from
  En-nadir, R. et al., J. Comput. Electron. 21 (2022) 111-118,
  doi:10.1007/s10825-021-01830-4

Structure: well 3 nm | barrier 3 nm | well 3 nm, centred in a 16 nm
domain. Conduction-band barrier V0 = 0.616 eV, from Eg(InN) = 0.78 eV,
Eg(GaN) = 3.42 eV, bowing b = 3.8 eV, x_In = 0.15 and a 70/30 band
offset. Constant effective mass m* = 0.19 m0, L = 1 nm, so the energy
unit is E = hbar^2/(2 m* L^2) = 0.2005 eV.

The well interfaces are deliberately placed off the mesh nodes: when an
interface coincides with a node the observed convergence order drops to
one (see Section 8.4 of the manuscript).

Run:
  gmsh 1D_ingan_gan_dqw.geo -2 -o 1D_ingan_gan_dqw.msh
  ElmerGrid 14 2 1D_ingan_gan_dqw.msh -out mesh
  ElmerSolver case.sif

Reference eigenvalues (n = 2560, dimensionless):
  E1 = 0.26689617    E2 = 0.33005601
